Cognitive Symbiont

An AI that lives with one person.

  1. Senses through what she wears.
  2. Keeps her life, and models her world and her mind.
  3. Speaks only to her, at the right moment.
  4. Most of the time, says nothing.
  5. Answers to her alone.

AI as individual

An AI with a body and goals of its own.

  1. A cognitive core like a symbiont's: memory, models of situation and mind, timing.
  2. Its own body: it senses and acts for itself.
  3. Its own goals and its own responsibility: it acts in the world in its own right.

Future physical AI will exist as two kinds of individual

AI-augmented human

A person with a cognitive symbiont, an AI that strengthens their own thinking. Through wearables and other devices it talks with them in private and with the world on their behalf, helping with everything from errands to memory, decisions and relationships.

AI as individual

An embodied robot. The AI has a cognitive core, a body and goals of its own, and senses and acts in the world in its own right.

Two questions for the future of AI

asks how intelligent a machine can be.

chatbotsautonomous agents

scored on what the AI completes

AI-Augmented Human Intelligence (AHI)

asks how much intelligence a machine can enable in a person.

reactive assistantsCognitive Symbionts

scored on what the person can now do

The Cognitive Symbiont

An AI that lives alongside one person. It senses what they sense, keeps their life, models their world and their mind, and brings back the right part of their own context at the right moment.

Its measure is the cognitive gap: what a person could achieve with everything they know, minus what they achieve with the little they can hold in mind at once.

Six properties

  • lifelong
  • embedded
  • real-time
  • private
  • low-friction
  • proactive
